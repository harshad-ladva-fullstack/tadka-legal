Tadka

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Tadka is a calorie tracker built for Indian and South Asian food. It is designed to work without an account: there is no sign-up, no cloud sync and no server that stores your food log. This page explains the few cases where data does leave your phone.

Short version: your meals, weight and profile stay on your device. A photo or text description is sent to an AI provider only when you tap Analyse. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. There is no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK in the app.

1. Data stored on your device

Tadka saves the following as JSON files in the app's private storage. It is not transmitted to us and we cannot read it:

  • Your profile: display name, sex, age, height, weight, goal and activity level.
  • Your food log: meals, portions, calories, macronutrients, dates and notes.
  • Your settings: theme, units, reminders and free-tier usage counters.

You can delete all of it at any time with Settings → Your data → Clear all data, or by uninstalling the app. Deleting the app removes the data permanently; there is no copy on our side to restore.

2. Food photos and descriptions

When you tap Analyse, the photo you selected or the text you typed is sent over HTTPS to our AI provider so it can estimate the nutrition. That request also includes nothing else about you — no name, no account, no location.

  • Provider: Groq, Inc. (United States), acting as our data processor. See groq.com/privacy-policy.
  • Purpose: producing the calorie and macro estimate shown in the app. We do not use it for advertising or model training.
  • Storage: we do not keep a copy. The estimate is written to your device only.
  • If the provider is unreachable, Tadka estimates on your device instead and nothing is sent.

Please avoid photographing people, documents or anything else you would not want processed by a third party.

3. Advertising

The free version of Tadka shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address, and general device and app information, and may use them to select and measure ads. See how Google uses information from partners.

  • On iOS we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before any personalised ad. If you decline, you get non-personalised ads.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings.
  • Tadka Pro removes ads entirely, and the ad SDK is not started at all for Pro users.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google. We never see your card details. The app receives only whether a subscription is active, its product identifier and its expiry date, and stores that on your device to unlock Pro.

5. What we do not do

  • No account, email address or phone number is required.
  • No analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK is included.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, microphone or health-platform data is read.
  • We do not sell personal data.

6. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies: processing your photo or description to produce an estimate is done to perform the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)). Showing personalised ads is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through the ad consent form or your device settings. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

7. Your rights

Because your log never reaches us, most requests are handled on your device: open Settings → Your data to export a JSON copy of everything or to delete it. For anything else — including access, correction, objection or a complaint — email harshad.ladva.fullstack@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Tadka is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you remove the data from the device.

9. Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Data on your device is protected by your phone's own encryption and screen lock. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device passcode enabled.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where the change affects you, show a notice in the app before it takes effect.
