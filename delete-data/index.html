Delete your Tadka data

Last updated 28 October 2026

Tadka has no account and no server-side copy of your food log, so deleting your data is something you can do completely on your own device.

Option 1 — Clear data inside the app

  1. Open Tadka.
  2. Go to Settings (the fourth tab).
  3. Under Your data, tap Clear all data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, every meal and day you have logged, and all of your settings.

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Tadka removes its private storage, which holds the same data. Nothing is left behind on any server of ours.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your display name, sex, age, height, weight, goal, activity level, food photos you selected, all logged meals and macros, and app settings — including the free-tier usage counter.
  • Kept by us: nothing. We do not operate a database of user data, so there is no copy to remove on our side.
  • Kept by our providers: Google may retain advertising and purchase records under its own retention rules (see the Privacy Policy). Apple and Google retain subscription records as payment processors. Food photos sent for AI analysis are not stored by us and are not used for training.
  • Exported files: if you used Export, the JSON file you saved is outside the app. Delete it from your Files app or computer.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email harshad.ladva.fullstack@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my data". Because we hold no server-side copy, we will confirm within 30 days that there is nothing to delete and help you remove the app's local storage.
